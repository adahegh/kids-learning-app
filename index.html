Splash & Learn

A free learning app for kids ages 4–5
Hi! 👋 I'm a parent, and I built this app to help my own kids practice writing, math, and reading — the same skills they're working on at school. It made practice feel like play for them, so I'm sharing it in the hope your family enjoys it too. It's completely free: no ads, no accounts, nothing to install.
▶ Start Playing
Works right in the browser — best on an iPad or phone 📱

What's inside

✏️
Writing
Trace uppercase and lowercase letters and numbers stroke by stroke, with animated guides showing how each one is drawn.
🔢
Math
Addition with "counting on", subtraction by crossing things out, and number bonds — with cute animals to tap and count.
💯
Counting
Count to 100 on a hundred chart, master the tricky 29→30 jumps, and count backwards to launch a rocket — every number speaks its name when tapped.
📖
Reading
The official Dolch sight words (Pre-K, Kindergarten, and Grade 1) in flashcards that speak, Star Catcher, and Word Pop bubbles — plus Spell It, a phonics game for hearing short vowel sounds in simple words.
🪞
Flip It
Practice for commonly reversed letters and numbers (b, d, p, q, C, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9) — trace them, spot the backwards ones, and sort sounds.

How kids stay motivated

🌊 Every game earns XP, and every 25 XP adds a fish to their aquarium. New sea creatures unlock as the tank grows — all the way to a mermaid 🧜‍♀️.

Notes for parents

  • 100% free — no ads, no purchases, no accounts
  • No personal data collected — progress is saved only on your device (anonymous page-view counts are the only stat)
  • Works in any browser; on an iPad or phone, use "Add to Home Screen" for a full-screen app feel
  • Turn the sound on — the reading games speak the words aloud
  • Aimed at ages 4–5 — basic math and letter tracing
